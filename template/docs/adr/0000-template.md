# N. Short decision title in the imperative

Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Status

Proposed

## Context

What is the issue we are seeing that motivates this decision or change? State the forces at
play — technical, organisational, and the constraints that bound the options.

## Decision

What is the change we are actually making? Use active voice: "We will ...".

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder because of this change? Include the costs accepted, the risks
taken on, and any follow-up work this creates. Record what is now **deferred** and the
**trigger** that would revisit it.
